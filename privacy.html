Summary

  • Scan analysis runs in your browser. The optional assistant uses an organisation-operated chat API.
  • Tenant data is read directly from Microsoft APIs into your browser. The assistant receives aggregate scan figures only when you choose to include them.
  • There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the browser session and are discarded when the session ends.
  • Reports you export are created and saved on your device.

The scan only performs read operations. The app changes your tenant in one case: when an administrator adds or removes a user's licences on the Licenses tab and confirms the change. That request goes directly from the browser to Microsoft Graph with the delegated permission LicenseAssignment.ReadWrite.All, which is requested only at that moment. The app never deletes users. Scan processing happens in the browser tab. The latest scan is kept in the tab's session storage so you can reopen it after a page refresh.

Sharing

The publisher does not receive, sell, rent, or share your data. The app communicates only with Microsoft endpoints that you authorise:

  • login.microsoftonline.com for sign-in
  • graph.microsoft.com for Microsoft 365 data
  • api.partner.microsoft.com for the Partner Center price sheet, only when a CSP partner selects Get prices from Partner Center

Your organisation's Microsoft Entra administrators control whether these permissions are granted. Microsoft's own privacy statement applies to data processed by those services.

Optional Azure OpenAI assistant

When you send a chat message, your organisation's chat API forwards it and recent messages to its Azure OpenAI model deployment in Microsoft Foundry. You can review and include aggregate scan figures with a message. The app keeps chat history in memory and clears it when you clear chat, close the page or change scans. The chat API does not store the conversation or log message content. Your organisation's Azure service settings and policies govern processing of messages.

Exports

CSV, JSON, and print/PDF exports are generated locally in your browser. They may contain personal data such as names, user principal names, departments, and licence assignments. You are responsible for storing and sharing those files according to your organisation's policies.

Children

LicenseLens is a business tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your controls

  • Disconnect clears the active account and returns the app to demo mode.
  • Sign out ends the Microsoft session for the app.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove saved settings, client/tenant IDs, and cached app files.
  • Ask your Microsoft 365 administrator to revoke the app's consent.

Security

The app uses OAuth 2.0 authorisation-code flow with PKCE. It does not use a client secret. Access tokens for the scan are held in session storage and used with Microsoft APIs. The assistant uses a separate access token held in browser memory and sent to its chat API.
